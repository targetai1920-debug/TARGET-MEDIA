Refund & Cancellation Policy

Quarterly service, clear exit rules.

This policy is designed for Target Media’s B2B quarterly service model. The signed proposal or order form can contain more specific terms and will control if there is a conflict.

Last updated: 9 September 2026
B2B scope: this policy is written for business customers. It does not attempt to waive rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. If Target Media later sells to consumers online, the consumer cancellation and withdrawal rules must be separately implemented before launch.

1. Before installation or service start

If the customer cancels before installation work or the paid service period has started, amounts paid for work that has not been performed should be refunded. Any genuinely non-recoverable third-party cost may only be deducted if that cost was disclosed or agreed in the written quote or order form.

2. Installation and initial configuration

Installation and initial configuration are priced separately. Amounts attributable to work already completed are not automatically refundable merely because the customer later changes its mind. Any unperformed portion should be handled according to the signed order form.

If specific hardware has been ordered or customised for the customer, cancellation treatment depends on whether it can reasonably be returned or reused and on the supplier terms disclosed in the quote.

3. Quarterly plan fees after service starts

Once a paid quarter has started and Target Media is providing the agreed service, the quarterly fee is generally non-refundable for convenience cancellation during that quarter.

This does not apply where the signed agreement gives a stronger right, where Target Media agrees otherwise in writing, or where a refund or remedy is required by applicable law.

4. If Target Media cannot provide the agreed service

If Target Media materially fails to provide the agreed paid service and does not cure the issue within any reasonable cure period stated in the contract, the customer may be entitled to an appropriate remedy under the signed agreement. Depending on the circumstances, this can include service credit, re-performance or a pro-rata refund for the affected unprovided portion.

If Target Media cancels a paid engagement without customer breach before the paid service is delivered, the customer should receive a refund for the undelivered portion, subject to the signed agreement and applicable law.

5. Technical limitations are not automatically a refund trigger

Camera analytics are affected by field of view, lighting, occlusion, weather, calibration, connectivity and model limitations. The service does not promise perfect detection accuracy or business outcomes. A limitation that was clearly disclosed and remains within the agreed scope is not, by itself, evidence that the service was not provided.

6. Renewal and non-renewal

The public website does not create automatic renewal. Renewal, notice periods and any termination date are defined in the written order form. If no renewal is agreed, the engagement ends at the end of the paid quarter.

7. How to request a refund or cancellation

The customer should use the formal contact details listed in its proposal, order form or contract and include the company name, plan, location and reason for the request. The public repository does not yet contain Target Media’s final registered contact information, so we do not invent an email address here.

8. Mandatory rights remain

Nothing in this policy excludes liability, remedies or statutory rights that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited. The signed agreement should be reviewed by Dutch counsel before the first paid rollout.

General contract reference: Business.gov.nl — agreements and contracts. Consumer distance-selling rules become relevant if the service is ever offered to consumers online.
