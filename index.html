Privacy-first design

Measure behavior, not identities.

Target Media is designed around anonymous, aggregated business metrics rather than identifying individual people.

Aggregate metricsDashboards focus on counts, rates and trends.
No customer profilesThe product is not built to create named shopper profiles.
Local contextBenchmarks are shown as aggregated references.
Business purposeDesigned to evaluate physical marketing performance.

Depending on the plan and installation, Target Media can measure traffic in front of the location, attention toward the storefront, stopping behavior and time-based patterns.
