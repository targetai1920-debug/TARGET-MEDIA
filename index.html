How it works

Measure the storefront. Interpret the signal.

The standard offer focuses on aggregated business metrics rather than identifying individuals. Actual deployments must be configured and documented for the specific location.

1

Measure

Estimate how many people pass, orient toward the storefront and stop, subject to camera placement, occlusion, lighting and model limitations.

Traffic → attention → stop
2

Understand

Track changes by hour, day and week, compare periods and add surrounding-area context where a lawful and technically valid benchmark exists.

Store → period → context
3

Act

Use quarterly summaries, alerts and data-backed recommendations as decision support. Metrics are estimates, not guaranteed causal explanations.

Signal → diagnosis → action

Deployment privacy: camera analytics can involve personal-data processing even when the final dashboard is aggregated. We do not market facial recognition, identity tracking, emotion recognition or demographic profiling in the standard plans. Before activation, the parties must document roles, lawful basis, necessity, signage, retention and whether a DPIA is required for the location.
