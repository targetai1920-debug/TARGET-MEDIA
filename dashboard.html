A quarterly summary should not feel cramped. This section gives the client a clear narrative view of what changed over the quarter, where the strongest performance came from, and how stopping behaviour evolved.

Traffic Higher than Q2 Passer-by volume increased 11.2% in this illustrative quarter, with the most meaningful lift concentrated in late afternoon.
